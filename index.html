About Me

Hi, I'm Prity. I'm currently pursuing my B.Sc. in Computer Science at Kazi Nazrul University, but my real education happens when I'm building things.

I don't like putting myself in a single box. I enjoy the logical challenge of backend development (PHP, Python, MySQL), the visual satisfaction of frontend design (CSS, Bootstrap), and the endless possibilities of Machine Learning. Whether I'm building a smart attendance system like ClassVibes or exploring AI for scam detection in SafeOnyx, my goal is always the same: to build software that actually solves everyday problems.
